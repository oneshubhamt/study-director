Versions
The Device Folder keeps verified versions in a folder you choose on this device; Google Drive keeps the same history off this device. Each version is a complete copy of your data, even though unchanged parts are stored once.
Several devices
While a device is signed in to Google Drive it syncs: it takes in what your other devices changed, then uploads its own changes. A change made on one side only is taken for you. Where both sides changed the same thing, the newer shows and the other is kept for you, marked Needs review; edits to different paragraphs of a note are joined for you. Saved data and the Active Session travel too; changes to a session running here wait until it ends. Without Drive, each device keeps working on its own.
Portable backup
Export backup creates one complete .sdbackup file for manual transfer or safekeeping. Restore it on any Study Director device when needed; it is not part of Version History.

Automatic backup

Your backups keep 15 versions: the latest, 6 daily, 4 weekly and 4 monthly, so about four to five months back. They also keep one version per hour from the last 24 hours when your notes changed, up to 10 more. Before a restore, each device also keeps a safety version for 7 days. The Device Folder and Google Drive keep the same history.

Work kind: work, study, writing, test, project
Support kind: support, gym, exercise, hobby, socialisation, admin
Fixed kind: fixed, meal, rest, sleep
Required: START, MINS, TASK, KIND, AREA. Optional: MEASURE and TARGET may be empty; use — for an unknown target if preferred.
Timeline: Follow the 4 a.m. study day in order. Overnight sequences such as 23:00 → 00:00 → 01:00 are valid. Work/support must end by 04:00; only a genuine fixed sleep/rest anchor may cross it. Overlaps or reversed rows are rejected; gaps are warnings.
Separators: Use either pipes or commas. Quote a field that contains a comma.
