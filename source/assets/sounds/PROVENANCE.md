# Quiet Wood — Study Director

Original compositions and generated acoustic-style samples created for this project.
No third-party recordings, instruments, samples, or sound libraries are incorporated.
These are rendered modal string/body synthesis, not recordings of a physical piano.

Reproduce with `python3 build/compose-sounds.py` (Python standard library only).
A shared D-major/pentatonic motif links beginning, settling, returning and resolving.
Felt-like attacks, damped upper modes, a quiet wooden body and short early reflections
replace the old runtime oscillator sweeps. Frequent gestures are deliberately shorter.
PCM mono 22.05 kHz, 16-bit; bounded peaks, matched RMS, and silent tails. The engine
adds short entry/exit fades. All cues are bundled and precached for offline playback.

The project owner may use, modify and redistribute these original assets with the app.
The manifest records reproducible signal measurements, not claims of listener testing.
